What We Do

Security Services

From a single post to a full security program, every assignment is planned around your site, your people and your risks.

Security Officers

Uniformed armed and unarmed officers for access control, front desk, overnight and dedicated posts.

Executive Protection

Discreet close protection for executives, VIPs and high-profile individuals, at home, at work or on the move.

Event Security

Crowd management, entry screening and VIP coverage for concerts, corporate events, weddings and private functions.

Mobile Patrol

Marked patrol vehicles, randomized site checks, lock-ups and alarm response with time-stamped reports.

Risk Assessments

On-site vulnerability reviews with a clear, prioritized plan to close gaps in people, process and technology.

Fire Watch & Loss Prevention

Fire watch coverage during system outages or hot work, plus loss prevention for retail and warehouse sites.

Editing your site

  • Click any outlined text and type to change it. Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I work for bold and italic.
  • Buttons & links: click into one, then press Edit link to change where it goes (e.g. tel:+15551234567, mailto:you@site.com, #contact).
  • Cards, steps & tags: use ↑ to move, ⧉ to duplicate, ✕ to delete, or + Add for a new one.
  • Images: use the image buttons on the logo, hero background and about photo.
  • Settings: brand color, page title, where each form sends, and the portal login.
  • Employee portal: use Staff logins to give each employee a username and password and a role. Supervisors sign in to the portal to edit the schedule, bulletin board and BOLOs.
  • Changes auto-save in this browser only. When you're happy, press Export HTML and upload the downloaded index.html to your web host.
